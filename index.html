Welcome to New Providence

It is the 1930s in New Providence, a fictional British Caribbean colony. Your group will help its people seek political rights, self-government and economic opportunities. Your decisions change your position on the road—but every choice teaches you real Caribbean history.

How to play: Work in a group on one device. Read the short stage briefing. Discuss each of six dilemmas, select one answer and see whether your token advances or retreats. Then read the Historical Discovery card and discuss its question before continuing. Discover five districts and face 30 decisions on the road to independence. Consult the History Vault when you need a clue.

Your adventure is saved on this device. No account required.

The Final Council

Discuss and answer: Assess the extent to which political enfranchisement and economic enfranchisement contributed to movements towards independence in the British Caribbean. Use at least two real Caribbean examples and distinguish adult suffrage, internal self-government and independence.

Keep a record of your adventure, discoveries and final council response.

New Providence is a fictional Caribbean colony. Historical events and examples refer to real Caribbean developments; constitutional journeys differed across territories.
